3. Concepts

These checks cover items 3 to 6 on pages 1 and 2 of the Section 3.1 notes and the Classroom Example 3.1.1 video on page 6.

Check 4 (Mean). Which description matches how the mean is calculated in item 3?
Show answer

Add all the data values and divide by the number of values.

Where this is: Item 3 (Mean) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 5 (Notation for the Mean). Which statement matches the notation for the mean in item 3?
Show answer

x̄ (x-bar) is the sample mean, and μ (mu) is the population mean.

Where this is: Item 3 (Mean) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 6 (Mean, Median and Outliers). A list of 9 home prices includes one mansion that costs far more than the other 8 homes. Which statement matches items 3 and 4?
Show answer

The mean is sensitive to this outlier, but the median is not affected by it.

Where this is: Items 3 and 4 (Mean, Median) on pages 1 and 2 of the notes.

Check 7 (Median). Which statement about the median matches item 4?
Show answer

It is the middle value after the data are put in order, written x̃ (x-tilde).

Where this is: Item 4 (Median) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 8 (Mode). A campus survey records each student's favorite pizza topping: pepperoni, cheese, sausage, and so on. Which measure of center from items 3 to 6 is useful for these data?
Show answer

The mode, because it is useful for categorical data.

Where this is: Item 5 (Mode) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 9 (Midrange Formula). Which one-line version of the midrange formula matches item 6?
Show answer

(maximum data value + minimum data value) / 2open parenthesis maximum data value plus minimum data value close parenthesis, slash 2

Where this is: Item 6 (Midrange) on page 2 and the Note on page 5 of the notes, and the video "Using StatCrunch to find measures of center" on page 3.

Check 10 (Midrange Advantages and Disadvantages). Which statement matches the advantages and disadvantages of the midrange in item 6?
Show answer

It is easy to compute without software, but very sensitive to outliers.

Where this is: Item 6 (Midrange) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 11 (Classroom Example 3.1.1). In the Classroom Example 3.1.1 video, the marriage proposal package costs have a median of 225 dollars and a midrange of 1,269.51,269 point 5 dollars. Which statement best explains the large gap between them?
Show answer

The outliers 2,000 and 2,500 pull up the midrange, but the median is not affected.

Where this is: Items 4 and 6 (Median, Midrange) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 3.1.1" on page 6.
