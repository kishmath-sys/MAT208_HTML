Check 1 (Point Estimate). A simple random sample of bike commute times has a sample standard deviation of s = 5 minutes, so the sample variance is s2s squared = 25. Which statement matches item 1?
Show answer

The best point estimate for the population variance σ2sigma squared is 25, the sample variance.

Where this is: Item 1 (Point Estimate) on page 1 of the notes.

The sample variance and critical values from the χ2chi-squared-distribution

Check 5 (As n Increases). Picture two χ2chi-squared distributions, one for a sample size of n = 5 and one for n = 40. Which statement matches item 3?
Show answer

The n = 40 distribution is less skewed than the n = 5 one.

Where this is: Item 3 (The Chi-Squared Distribution) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 6 (Requirements). A researcher has a simple random sample of 60 home sale prices and wants a confidence interval for σsigma. Home sale prices in this population are strongly right-skewed, not normal. Which statement matches item 4?
Show answer

The requirements are not met, because the population must follow a normal distribution.

Where this is: Item 4 (Requirements for Estimating a Population Standard Deviation) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 7 (Phase I Statement). A 95% confidence interval for the population standard deviation of pizza delivery times is 2.4 minutes to 5.1 minutes. Which Phase I statement follows the pattern in item 5?
Show answer

We are 95% confident that the population standard deviation of delivery times is between 2.4 minutes and 5.1 minutes.

Where this is: Item 5 (Writing Confidence Interval Conclusions) on page 2 of the notes, and the Phase I statement on page 7.

Check 8 (Answering the Question). A study of a new plant fertilizer finds a 90% confidence interval of 1.8 cm to 3.6 cm for the population standard deviation of plant heights. A reporter asks, "Does this show that the fertilizer makes plants grow taller?" Which answer matches the notes and the Classroom Example 7.3.1 video?
Show answer

No, because the interval estimates variability, not whether plants grew taller.

Where this is: Item 5 (Writing Confidence Interval Conclusions) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 7.3.1" on page 8.

Check 9 (Context of the Sample). A simple random sample of drive-through wait times was collected on Saturdays between 8 and 9 a.m. The 95% confidence interval for the population standard deviation of wait times is 0.9 minutes to 2.1 minutes. Does this interval describe wait times for all hours of the week?
Show answer

No, because the sample only came from Saturdays between 8 and 9 a.m.

Where this is: Item 5 (Writing Confidence Interval Conclusions) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 7.3.2" on page 8.
