2. Key Definitions and Requirements

These checks cover the Key Definitions and topic 1 (Requirements) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 1 (Dependent Samples). A fitness coach records the resting heart rate of 15 clients in January, then records it again for the same 15 clients in June. Which description matches the Key Definitions?
Show answer

Dependent samples, because each January reading is paired with the same client's June reading.

Where this is: The Key Definitions on page 1 of the notes.

Check 2 (Independent Samples). A researcher records the commute times of 40 randomly selected workers in Chicago and of 40 randomly selected workers in Denver. Which description matches the Key Definitions?
Show answer

Independent samples, because no Chicago worker is paired with a Denver worker.

Where this is: The Key Definitions on page 1 of the notes.

Check 3 (Requirements). A matched pairs study has 12 pairs. Which statement matches requirement C in topic 1?
Show answer

Since 12 is not more than 30, the population distributions must be normal.

Where this is: Topic 1 (Requirements) on page 1 of the notes.
