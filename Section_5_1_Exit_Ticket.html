4. Significantly High or Low

These checks cover item 5 on page 4 of the Section 5.1 notes and Classroom Example 5.1.4 on page 6.

Check 7 (Significantly High). For some number of successes x, the probability of getting x or more successes is 0.0120 point 0 1 2, and the probability of exactly x successes is 0.0090 point 0 0 9. Which statement matches item 5?
Show answer

Significantly high, because the probability of x or more is 0.050 point 0 5 or less.

Where this is: Item 5 (Significantly High or Significantly Low Number of Successes) on page 4 of the notes.

Check 8 (Exact and Cumulative Probability). A gardener plants 8 seeds. Which probability is relevant for deciding whether 2 seeds sprouting is a significantly low number?
Show answer

The probability of 2 or fewer
