Check 3 (Step One). A college claims that the proportion of online students who finish a course is greater than the proportion of in-person students who finish. Population 1 is online students and population 2 is in-person students. Which pair is correct for Step One?

Check 4 (Labeling the Populations). Classroom Example 9.1.1 claims the incidence of malaria is lower for infants using bednets. Suppose you instead let population 1 be infants not using bednets and population 2 be infants using bednets. Which alternative hypothesis states the claim?

Check 5 (Steps Two and Four). A problem says to use a 0.01 significance level, and StatCrunch gives a p-value of 0.00890 point 0 0 8 9. Which decision matches Step Four?
