Check 6 (Units of Measurement). A researcher finds r = 0.810 point 8 1 for the heights (inches) and weights (pounds) of 50 adults. She changes the heights to centimeters and the weights to kilograms and finds r again. What does item 2 say she will get?
Show answer

r = 0.810 point 8 1 again, because r does not change when the units change.

Where this is: Item 2 (Linear Correlation Coefficient) and its Properties of r on page 3 of the notes.

Check 7 (Only Linear Relationships). A scatterplot of a car's speed (miles per hour) and its gas mileage (miles per gallon) shows points that rise and then fall in a clear curve, and r is close to 0. Which statement matches item 2?
Show answer

There is no linear correlation, and r does not measure the curved relationship.

Where this is: Item 2 (Linear Correlation Coefficient) and its Properties of r on page 3 of the notes.

Check 8 (Outliers). A scatterplot of 15 points has r = 0.120 point 1 2. One more point, far away from all the others, is added, and r becomes 0.680 point 6 8. Which statement matches item 2?
Show answer

r is sensitive to outliers, so one point far from the rest can change r a lot.

Where this is: Item 2 (Linear Correlation Coefficient) and its Properties of r on page 3 of the notes.
