2. Sampling Distributions and the Sample Mean

These checks cover the Introduction and items 1 and 2 on page 1 of the Section 6.3 notes.

Check 1 (Sampling Distribution of a Statistic). Which description matches item 1's definition of a sampling distribution?
Show answer

The distribution of a statistic from all possible samples of size n.

Where this is: The Introduction and item 1 (Sampling Distribution of a Statistic) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 2 (Which Statistics). Item 1 lists three statistics whose sampling distributions this section explores. Which list matches?
Show answer

Sample mean (x̄x bar), sample variance (s2s squared) and sample proportion (p̂p hat).

Where this is: Item 1 (Sampling Distribution of a Statistic) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 3 (Why Sampling Distributions Matter). According to item 1, what does understanding sampling distributions allow us to do?
Show answer

Estimate probabilities and test hypotheses.

Where this is: Item 1 (Sampling Distribution of a Statistic) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 4 (Sample Means Target the Population Mean). A store manager takes many random samples of 40 receipts and computes the mean of each sample. Which statement matches the first property in item 2?
Show answer

Each sample mean approximates the population mean.

Where this is: Item 2 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 5 (Shape for Sample Means). When all of the sample means from samples of the same size are placed into a distribution, what does item 2 say about it?
Show answer

It is approximately a normal distribution.

Where this is: Item 2 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 6 (Practical Use for Sample Means). What does the Practical Use in item 2 say the properties of sample means let us do?
Show answer

Treat a sample mean as a single data point in a normal distribution.

Where this is: Item 2 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean) on page 1 of the notes, including its Practical Use.

If any of these felt shaky

  • Sampling Distribution of a Statistic: item 1, page 1
  • Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean: item 2, page 1 (and its Practical Use)

3. Sample Variances and Sample Proportions

These checks cover items 3 and 4 on pages 1 and 2 of the Section 6.3 notes.

Check 7 (Sample Variances Target the Population Variance). Which statement about sample variances matches the first property in item 3?
Show answer

Each sample variance approximates the population variance.

Where this is: Item 3 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Variance) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 8 (Shape for Sample Variances). How does item 3 describe the distribution of the sample variances?
Show answer

Typically right-skewed.

Where this is: Item 3 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Variance) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 9 (Reason for the Shape). What reason does item 3 give for the shape of the distribution of sample variances?
Show answer

A variance is a squared value, which amplifies deviations into higher values.

Where this is: Item 3 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Variance) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 10 (Notation for Proportions). In the Notation for item 4, what do p̂p hat and p stand for?
Show answer

p̂p hat is the sample proportion, and p is the population proportion.

Where this is: Item 4 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Proportion) on page 2 of the notes, including its Notation.

Check 11 (Properties of Sample Proportions). A polling group takes many random samples of 500 voters and computes p̂p hat for each sample. Which statement matches the properties in item 4?
Show answer

Each p̂p hat approximates p, and the distribution is approximately normal.

Where this is: Item 4 (Sampling Distribution of the Sample Proportion) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 12 (Which Are Approximately Normal). Across items 2 to 4, which statistics have a sampling distribution that the notes describe as approximately normal?
Show answer

Sample means and sample proportions.

Where this is: Items 2 to 4 on pages 1 and 2 of the notes.

If any of these felt shaky

  • Sampling Distribution of the Sample Variance: item 3, page 1
  • Sampling Distribution of the Sample Proportion: item 4, page 2 (and its Notation and Practical Use)

4. Biased vs. Unbiased Estimators

These checks cover item 5 on page 2 of the Section 6.3 notes.

Check 13 (Biased Estimator). Which statement matches item 5A's definition of a biased statistic?
Show answer

It does not target its parameter, so its expected value does not equal the parameter.

Where this is: Item 5A (Biased Estimator) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 14 (Examples of Biased Estimators). Which statistic is listed in item 5A as a biased estimator?
Show answer

The sample median (x̃x tilde)

Where this is: Item 5A (Biased Estimator) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 15 (Unbiased Estimators). Which pairing of a statistic and the parameter it targets is an unbiased estimator in item 5?
Show answer

The sample variance (s2s squared) targets σ2sigma squared.

Where this is: Items 5A (Biased Estimator) and 5B (Unbiased Estimator) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 16 (Important Note on s). Which statement matches the Important Note at the end of item 5?
Show answer

s is biased for σsigma but still commonly used, especially when samples are large.

Where this is: The Important Note in item 5 (Biased vs. Unbiased Estimators) on page 2 of the notes.

If any of these felt shaky

  • Biased Estimator: item 5A, page 2
  • Unbiased Estimator: item 5B, page 2
  • Important Note about the sample standard deviation: item 5, page 2
