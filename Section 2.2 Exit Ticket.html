Check 1 (Axes of a histogram). A histogram shows the commute times, in minutes, of 60 employees. Which statement matches item 1?
Show answer

The horizontal axis shows the class boundaries of the commute times, and the vertical axis shows the frequency.

Where this is: Item 1 (Histogram) on page 1 of the notes, including its Important Notes.

Check 2 (Axis labels). For the same commute time histogram, which pair of axis labels follows the Important Notes for item 1?
Show answer

Horizontal axis: Commute time (minutes). Vertical axis: Frequency.

Where this is: Item 1 (Histogram) on page 1 of the notes, including its Important Notes.

Check 3 (Bar width and height). In the commute time histogram, one bar runs from 20 minutes to 30 minutes and has a height of 14. Which statement matches item 1?

The bar's width matches the class width, 10 minutes, and its height means 14 commute times fall in that class.

Where this is: Item 1 (Histogram) on page 1 of the notes, including its Important Notes.

3. Normal, Skewed, and Uniform Distributions

These checks cover items 2 to 5 on pages 4 and 5 of the notes and Classroom Example 2.2.1 on page 6.

Check 5 (Shape of the heights). A histogram of the heights of a group of adults has bars with these heights from left to right: 2, 6, 13, 20, 14, 7, 2. Which shape does it have?
Show answer

Normal

Where this is: Item 2 (Normal Distribution) on page 4 of the notes.

Check 6 (Shape of the last digits). A histogram of the last digit of 600 phone numbers has 10 bars. Every bar has a height between 55 and 64, and there is no clear peak. Which statement matches item 3?
Show answer

It is uniform, because each class has about the same frequency.

Where this is: Item 3 (Uniform Distribution) on page 4 of the notes.

Check 7 (Shape of the hold times). A histogram of how long callers waited on hold has bars with these heights from left to right: 24, 18, 10, 6, 3, 1. Which description fits?
Show answer

Skewed to the right, because the tail points to the right.

Where this is: Item 4 (Skewed to the Right) on page 5 of the notes.

Check 8 (Shape of the quiz scores). A histogram of scores on an easy quiz has bars with these heights from left to right: 1, 2, 4, 9, 17, 23. Which description fits?
Show answer

Skewed to the left, because the tail points to the left.

Where this is: Item 5 (Skewed to the Left) on page 5 of the notes.

Check 9 (Classroom Example 2.2.1). In the "Classroom Example 2.2.1" video, the histogram of the 45 drive-through service times has a longer right tail. Does the histogram appear to be normal?
Show answer

No. It is skewed to the right, so it does not appear to be normal.

Where this is: Classroom Example 2.2.1 on page 6 of the notes, the video "Classroom Example 2.2.1" on page 6, and items 2 and 4 on pages 4 and 5.
