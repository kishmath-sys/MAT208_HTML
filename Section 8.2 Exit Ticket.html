Check 9 (Steps Two and Four). A problem says to use a 0.01 significance level, and StatCrunch gives a p-value of 0.03000 point 0 3 0 0. Which decision is correct?
Show answer

DNR H0H sub 0, because 0.03000 point 0 3 0 0 is greater than 0.010 point 0 1.

Where this is: Steps Two and Four of the Example on page 2 of the notes.

Check 10 (Step Four). With α = 0.05alpha equals 0 point 0 5, StatCrunch gives a p-value of 0.04180 point 0 4 1 8. Which decision is correct?
Show answer

Reject H0H sub 0, because 0.04180 point 0 4 1 8 is less than or equal to 0.050 point 0 5.

Where this is: Step Four of the Example on page 2 of the notes.

Check 11 (Step Five). In the test of the claim that more than 40% of drivers use a phone app for directions (Check 5), the decision is Reject H0H sub 0. Which conclusion matches the table on page 3?
Show answer

There is sufficient evidence to support the claim that more than 40% of drivers use a phone app.

Where this is: Step Five of the Example and its conclusion table on page 3 of the notes.

Check 12 (Step Five with the claim as the null). In the bakery test of the claim that 30% of customers buy bread (Check 8), the decision is DNR H0H sub 0. Which conclusion matches the table on page 3?
Show answer

There is not sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that 30% of customers buy bread.

Where this is: Step Five of the Example and its conclusion table on page 3 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 8.2.3" on page 5.

Check 13 (Step Five with DNR). A test of the claim that fewer than half of adults have a will ends with the decision DNR H0H sub 0. Which conclusion matches the table on page 3?
