Check 6 (The Required Step). A homework problem says, "Find the regression equation and the best predicted value of y when x = 12." It says nothing about correlation. What do the notes say comes first?
Show answer

Test for evidence of a linear correlation, even though the directions do not say to.

Where this is: Item 2 (Predicting Values of y) on page 1 and the REQUIRED STEP on pages 2 and 5 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 10.2.1" on page 7.

Check 7 (Which Prediction to Use). A test finds no evidence of a linear correlation between x and y. According to item 2, what is the best prediction for y?
Show answer

The mean of the y-values.

Where this is: Item 2 (Predicting Values of y) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 8 (Reading the Decision). In the Classroom Example 10.2.1 video, the test gives a P-value of 0.36970 point 3 6 9 7 with a significance level of 0.050 point 0 5. Which statement matches the decision and the prediction method used?
Show answer

Do not reject H₀H sub 0, so there is no evidence of a linear correlation, and the mean of the y-values is used.

Where this is: Item 2 (Predicting Values of y) on page 1 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 10.2.1" on page 7.
