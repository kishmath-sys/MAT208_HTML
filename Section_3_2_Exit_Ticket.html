2. Range, Standard Deviation and Variance

These checks cover the Introductory Example and items 1 to 3 on pages 1 and 2 of the Section 3.2 notes, and the Classroom Example 3.2.2 video on page 7.

Check 1 (Introductory Example). Two coffee machines each fill five cups. Machine A pours 8, 8, 8, 8 and 8 ounces. Machine B pours 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 ounces. Both have a mean of 8 ounces and a median of 8 ounces. Which statement matches the Introductory Example?
Show answer

The centers match, but Machine B varies more, which the mean and median do not show.

Where this is: The Introductory Example on page 1 of the notes.

Check 2 (Range). Five runners logged these distances in miles last week: 14.214 point 2, 9.89 point 8, 21.521 point 5, 17.017 point 0, 12.612 point 6. Find the range in miles. Do not round.

Show answer

11.711 point 7 miles

Where this is: Item 1 (Range) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 3 (Outliers). Monthly rents, in dollars, for eight apartments are 850, 875, 900, 910, 925, 950, 4,800 and 6,200. Are there any outliers, and are they likely to have much of an effect on the measures of variation?
Show answer

Yes, the largest amounts appear to be outliers, and they are likely to have a large effect on the measures of variation.

Where this is: Items 1 and 2 (Range, Standard Deviation) on page 1 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.2" on page 7.

Check 4 (Standard Deviation of Identical Values). A store weighs 6 bags of rice, and every bag weighs exactly 2.52 point 5 pounds. What is the standard deviation of these weights?
Show answer

0 pounds

Where this is: Items 2 and 3 (Standard Deviation, Variance) on pages 1 and 2 of the notes.

Check 5 (Units). Commute times for a sample of workers are measured in minutes, and the standard deviation is 6.26 point 2 minutes. Which statement about units matches items 2 and 3?
Show answer

The standard deviation is in minutes, and the variance is in minutes squared.

Where this is: Items 2 and 3 (Standard Deviation, Variance) on pages 1 and 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.2" on page 7.

Check 6 (Variance from Standard Deviation). The standard deviation of a set of package weights is 4.54 point 5 kilograms. Find the variance. Do not round.

Show answer

20.2520 point 2 5 kilograms squared

Where this is: Item 3 (Variance) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 7 (Notation). A report gives σ² = 36sigma squared equals 36 for an entire population. What does σ²sigma squared name?
Show answer

The population variance

Where this is: The Notations under items 2 and 3 on pages 1 and 2 of the notes, and item 7 (Unbiased Estimator) on page 3.

3. Range Rule of Thumb

These checks cover item 4 on page 2 of the notes and the Classroom Example 3.2.1 and 3.2.3 videos on page 7.

Check 8 (Range Rule of Thumb). By the range rule of thumb in item 4, about what percent of the data values fall within two standard deviations of the mean?
Show answer

About 95%

Where this is: Item 4 (Range Rule of Thumb) on page 2 of the notes.

Check 9 (Minimum Usual Value). Heights of a sample of adult women have a mean of 172.45172 point 4 5 cm and a standard deviation of 8.378 point 3 7 cm. Find the minimum usual value. If rounding is necessary, then round to one decimal place.

Show answer

155.7155 point 7 cm

Where this is: Item 4 (Range Rule of Thumb) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.3" on page 7.

Check 10 (Maximum Usual Value). For the same heights (mean 172.45172 point 4 5 cm, standard deviation 8.378 point 3 7 cm), find the maximum usual value. If rounding is necessary, then round to one decimal place.

Show answer

189.2189 point 2 cm

Where this is: Item 4 (Range Rule of Thumb) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.1" on page 7.

Check 11 (Significantly High). Weights of newborn puppies of one breed have a mean of 412.5412 point 5 grams and a standard deviation of 37.837 point 8 grams. The limits from the range rule of thumb are 336.9336 point 9 grams and 488.1488 point 1 grams. Would a weight of 470.0470 point 0 grams be significantly high?
Show answer

No, because it is between the two limits.

Where this is: Item 4 (Range Rule of Thumb) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.1" on page 7.

Check 12 (Significantly Low, High or Neither). Resting pulse rates for a sample of 100 adult women have a mean of 74.374 point 3 beats per minute and a standard deviation of 11.611 point 6 beats per minute. Is a pulse rate of 49.049 point 0 beats per minute significantly low, significantly high, or neither?
Show answer

Significantly low, because it is below the minimum usual value of 51.151 point 1 beats per minute.

Where this is: Item 4 (Range Rule of Thumb) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.3" on page 7.

4. Estimating Standard Deviation and Estimators

These checks cover items 5 to 7 on page 3 of the Section 3.2 notes.

Check 13 (Estimating Standard Deviation). In a reaction-time study, the maximum data value is 88.488 point 4 seconds and the minimum is 51.651 point 6 seconds. Estimate the standard deviation. If rounding is necessary, then round to one decimal place.

Show answer

9.29 point 2 seconds

Where this is: Item 5 (Estimating Standard Deviation) on page 3 of the notes.

Check 14 (Biased Estimator). A forester uses the sample standard deviation s from 30 trees to estimate the population standard deviation σsigma for the whole forest. Which statement matches item 6?
Show answer

The sample standard deviation is a biased estimator, because it typically differs from σsigma.

Where this is: Item 6 (Biased Estimator) on page 3 of the notes.

Check 15 (Unbiased Estimator). Which sample measurement does item 7 list as tending to equal its population measurement?
Show answer

The sample variance, s²s squared

Where this is: Item 7 (Unbiased Estimator) on page 3 of the notes.
