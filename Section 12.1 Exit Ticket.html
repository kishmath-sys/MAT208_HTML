These checks cover items 1 to 3 on page 1 of the Section 12.1 notes, and the Notes on Step One on page 2.

Check 1 (One-Way ANOVA). Which statement matches the description of one-way ANOVA in item 1?
Show answer

It tests whether three or more population means are equal, based on one factor.

Where this is: Item 1 (One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 2 (Factor). A company records the commute time (in minutes) of 60 employees who drive, take the bus or ride a bike, to compare the mean commute times. In the sense of item 1, what is the factor (also called the treatment)?
Show answer

The way each employee commutes

Where this is: Item 1 (One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 3 (Requirements). Which of these is not one of the requirements listed in item 2?
Show answer

Each sample has at least 30 values.

Where this is: Item 2 (Requirements for One-Way ANOVA) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 4 (Only One Factor). A study compares mean plant growth for three fertilizers and, in the same analysis, two watering schedules. Which requirement from item 2 does this plan break?
Show answer

There is only one factor being studied.

Where this is: Item 2 (Requirements for One-Way ANOVA) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 5 (Null Hypothesis). A study compares the mean battery life of four brands of phones. Which null hypothesis follows the form in item 3?
Show answer

H0H sub 0: μ1 = μ2 = μ3 = μ4mu sub 1 equals mu sub 2 equals mu sub 3 equals mu sub 4

Where this is: Item 3 (Hypotheses for One-Way ANOVA) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 6 (Alternative Hypothesis). For three population means, which alternative hypothesis is written the way the notes require?
Show answer

H1H sub 1: at least one of the means is different

Where this is: Item 3 (Hypotheses for One-Way ANOVA) and its Note on page 1, and the Notes on Step One on page 2 of the notes.

Check 9 (Decision). In Step Three a test gives F = 1.421 point 4 2 and p-value = 0.2640 point 2 6 4, and αalpha = 0.050 point 0 5. Which Step Four decision follows the notes?
Show answer

Do not reject H0H sub 0, since 0.264 is larger than 0.05

Where this is: Step Four of item 4 and its Notes on page 3 of the notes.

Check 10 (Decision). A test gives p-value = 0.0030 point 0 0 3 with αalpha = 0.010 point 0 1. Which Step Four decision follows the notes?
Show answer

Reject H0H sub 0, since 0.003 is less than 0.01

Where this is: Step Four of item 4 and its Notes on page 3 of the notes.

Check 11 (Conclusion). Go back to the clinic claim in Check 7. Suppose the Step Four decision is Do Not Reject H0H sub 0. Which Step Five conclusion follows the table on page 3?
Show answer

"There is not sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the mean wait times at the three clinics are the same."

Where this is: Step Five of item 4, its Notes and the conclusion table on page 3 of the notes.
