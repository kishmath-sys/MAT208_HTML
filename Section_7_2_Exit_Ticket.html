1. How this works

This exit ticket is ungraded and private. No one sees your answers unless you choose to send them to your instructor, Matt Read. It has 17 short checks and takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Do it after you have gone through the Section 7.2 notes and videos.
  • Each check tells you right away whether you have it. If not, it names the item, page and video to go back to.
  • Have StatCrunch open for Section 5.
  • Section 6 gives you a summary and a short message you can send to Matt.
  • Your answers save on this device, so you can stop and pick up later.
