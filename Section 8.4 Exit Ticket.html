  • Section 6 gives you a summary and a short message you can send to Matt.
  • Your answers save on this device, so you can stop and pick up later.

Check 6 (Step One with "Decreased"). After a filling machine is adjusted, a manager claims the standard deviation of fill weights has decreased from 0.8 ounce. Which alternative hypothesis matches this claim?

Check 10 (Step Four with α = 0.01). A test is run at the 0.01 significance level, like the example on pages 4 to 6. The p-value is 0.023. Which decision matches Step Four?
Show answer

DNR H0H sub 0, because 0.023 > 0.010 point 0 2 3 is greater than 0 point 0 1.

Where this is: Step Four and its notes on page 5 of the notes.

Check 11 (Step Five after Reject). The claim "the standard deviation of waiting times is less than 2 minutes" was written as the alternative, and the decision was Reject H0H sub 0. Which conclusion matches the table on page 6?

Where this is: Step Five and the conclusion table on page 6 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 8.4.2" on page 8.
