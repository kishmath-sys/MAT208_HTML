A short, ungraded check to use after the Section 8.3 notes and videos.


1. How this works

This exit ticket is ungraded and private. No one sees your answers unless you choose to send them to your instructor, Matt Read. It has 19 short checks and takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Do it after you have gone through the Section 8.3 notes and videos.
  • Each check tells you right away whether you have it. If not, it names the item, page and video to go back to.
  • Have StatCrunch open for Section 5.
  • Section 6 gives you a summary and a short message you can send to Matt.
  • Your answers save on this device, so you can stop and pick up later.

Go in order. About 10 to 15 minutes.

4. Steps Four and Five

These checks cover Steps Four and Five on pages 2, 3 and 5 of the notes and the Classroom Example 8.3.1 and 8.3.2 videos on page 7.

Check 10 (Step Four, Comparing). In Step Two, α = 0.01alpha equals 0 point 0 1. In Step Three, the p-value is 0.06130 point 0 6 1 3. Which decision matches Step Four?
Show answer

Do not reject H0H sub 0, because 0.06130 point 0 6 1 3 is greater than 0.010 point 0 1.

Where this is: Step Four and the Notes on Step Four on pages 2 and 5 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 8.3.2" on page 7.

Check 11 (Step Four, Equal Values). In Step Two, α = 0.05alpha equals 0 point 0 5. In Step Three, the p-value is 0.050 point 0 5, the same as αalpha. Which decision matches the Notes on Step Four?
Show answer

Reject H0H sub 0, because the p-value is less than or equal to αalpha.

Where this is: The Notes on Step Four on pages 2 and 5 of the notes.

Check 12 (Step Five, Claim in the Null). Step One is H0: μ = 12 (claim)H sub 0: mu equals 12, claim, H1: μ ≠ 12H sub 1: mu is not equal to 12, for the claim that the mean weight of a bag of rice is 12 pounds. Step Four is Do Not Reject H0H sub 0. Which conclusion matches the Step Five table?
Show answer

There is not sufficient evidence to warrant rejection of the claim that the mean weight of a bag of rice is 12 pounds.

Where this is: Step Five and its table on page 3 of the notes (the table from topic 6 in the Section 8.1 notes).

Check 13 (Step Five, Claim in the Alternative, Reject). Step One is H0: μ = 20H sub 0: mu equals 20, H1: μ > 20 (claim)H sub 1: mu is greater than 20, claim, for the claim that the mean wait time at a clinic is greater than 20 minutes. Step Four is Reject H0H sub 0. Which conclusion matches the Step Five table?
Show answer

There is sufficient evidence to support the claim that the mean wait time at a clinic is greater than 20 minutes.

Where this is: The Step Five table on pages 3 and 5 of the notes, and the video "Classroom Example 8.3.1" on page 7.

Check 14 (Step Five, Claim in the Alternative, DNR). Step One is H0: μ = 250H sub 0: mu equals 250, H1: μ < 250 (claim)H sub 1: mu is less than 250, claim, for the claim that the mean number of calories in a snack bar is less than 250. Step Four is Do Not Reject H0H sub 0. Which conclusion matches the Step Five table?

There is no evidence to support the claim that the mean number of calories in a snack bar is less than 250.
