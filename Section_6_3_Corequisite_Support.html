2. Squares and square roots

What you will be able to do

  • Read a small raised 2 as "squared," so 727 squared is 7 times 7.
  • Square a negative number and get a positive result.
  • Tell (−5)2open parenthesis negative 5 close parenthesis squared apart from −52negative 5 squared, with no parentheses.
  • Square a decimal and place the decimal point correctly.
  • Find a square root, the number that was squared.

Squared means times itself

A small raised 2 is an exponent. It means multiply the number by itself, and it is read "squared." So 727 squared is 7 × 7 = 497 times 7 equals 49, not 7 × 27 times 2.

  • Negative numbers: a negative times a negative is positive, so (−4)2 = (−4) × (−4) = 16open parenthesis negative 4 close parenthesis squared equals negative 4 times negative 4, which equals 16. A square is never negative.
  • Parentheses matter: −42negative 4 squared, with no parentheses squares only the 4 and then puts the minus sign in front: −(4 × 4) = −16negative, open parenthesis 4 times 4 close parenthesis, equals negative 16. Many calculators read −4^2negative 4, caret, 2 this way too. Use parentheses to square a negative number.
  • Decimals: count the digits after the point in the number, and the square has twice as many. 0.32 = 0.3 × 0.3 = 0.090 point 3 squared equals 0 point 3 times 0 point 3, which equals 0 point 0 9, not 0.90 point 9.
  • Square roots: the square root sign √square root asks which non-negative number was squared. √49 = 7the square root of 49 equals 7 because 72 = 497 squared equals 49. Square roots undo squaring.

Three slips to watch for

  • Doubling instead of squaring. 626 squared is 36, not 12. The 2 says how many times the number is used as a factor, not what to multiply by.
  • Keeping the negative sign. (−6)2open parenthesis negative 6 close parenthesis squared is 36, not negative 36. Two negatives multiply to a positive.
  • Losing a decimal place. 0.420 point 4 squared is 0.160 point 1 6. One digit after the point, squared, gives two digits after the point.

Worked example: Square −1.2negative 1 point 2

Find (−1.2)2open parenthesis negative 1 point 2 close parenthesis squared.

  1. (−1.2) × (−1.2)negative 1 point 2 times negative 1 point 2Squared means the number times itself. Keep the parentheses so the negative sign is part of the number.
  2. 12 × 12 = 14412 times 12 equals 144Multiply without the decimal points first.
  3. 1.441 point 4 4Each factor has one digit after the point, so the answer has two. A negative times a negative is positive.

Answer: 1.441 point 4 4 (not −1.44negative 1 point 4 4, and not −2.4negative 2 point 4, which doubles)

3. What squaring does to a number

What you will be able to do

  • Predict whether squaring makes a number larger, smaller or the same.
  • See that squaring stretches larger numbers much more than smaller ones.
  • Compare the squares of two numbers, including a negative number.
  • Use the fact that a square is never negative.

Bigger, smaller or the same

Many people expect multiplying to always make a number bigger. Squaring shows that is not always true.

Some numbers and their squares
NumberSquareWhat happened
0.50 point 50.250 point 2 5Smaller
11Same
24Larger by 2
10100Larger by 90
−10negative 10100Becomes positive and larger
  • Between 0 and 1: squaring makes the number smaller, because you take a part of a part.
  • Greater than 1: squaring makes the number larger, and the larger the number, the bigger the jump. 2 grows to 4, but 10 grows to 100.
  • Negative: the square is positive. A square is never negative.
  • Times as large: if one number is 3 times another, its square is 9 times the other square, because 3 times 3 is 9.

Two slips to watch for

  • "Multiplying always makes bigger." 0.82 = 0.640 point 8 squared equals 0 point 6 4, which is smaller than 0.80 point 8.
  • Thinking the gap stays the same. 8 is 2 times 4, but 64 is 4 times 16. When one number is a few times another, the squares are much further apart.

Worked example: Compare 3 and 12 after squaring

12 is 4 times as large as 3. How many times as large is the square of 12 as the square of 3?

  1. 32 = 9 and 122 = 1443 squared equals 9, and 12 squared equals 144Square each number.
  2. 144 ÷ 9 = 16144 divided by 9 equals 16Divide to compare. Notice that 16 is 4 times 4.

Answer: 16 times as large, not 4 times

4. Part of a whole as a fraction, decimal or percent

What you will be able to do

  • Pick out the whole group and the part in a sentence.
  • Write the part of the whole as a fully simplified fraction, a decimal and a percent.
  • Know that a part of a whole, as a decimal, is between 0 and 1.
  • Compare parts of a whole written in different forms.

Part divided by whole

To describe a part of a whole group, divide the part by the whole. The result is a decimal from 0 to 1. Multiply by 100 to write it as a percent.

part ÷ whole = decimal, then decimal × 100 = percentpart divided by whole equals a decimal, then the decimal times 100 equals the percent

  • The whole is the entire group. It often comes after words such as "of the," "out of" or "in a group of."
  • The part is the smaller group the question asks about. A part can never be more than the whole, so the decimal can never be more than 1.
  • To simplify a fraction, divide the top and bottom by the largest number that goes into both. Keep going until no number other than 1 goes into both.

Three slips to watch for

  • Flipping the division. Whole divided by part gives a number over 1, which cannot describe a part of a whole.
  • Using the other part as the whole. If 14 of 40 books are late, the other 26 are not the whole. The whole is all 40.
  • Stopping a simplification early. 6/156 over 15 can still be divided by 3. It simplifies to 2/52 over 5.

Worked example: Seeds that sprouted

A gardener planted 250 seeds, and 215 of them sprouted. Write the part that sprouted as a fraction, a decimal and a percent.

  1. Part: 215 sprouted. Whole: 250 planted.Label both numbers before dividing.
  2. 215/250 = 43/50215 over 250 equals 43 over 50Simplify. 5 goes into both 215 and 250. No number other than 1 goes into both 43 and 50.
  3. 215 ÷ 250 = 0.86215 divided by 250 equals 0 point 8 6Divide the part by the whole for the decimal. It is between 0 and 1, as it should be.
  4. 0.86 × 100 = 860 point 8 6 times 100 equals 86Multiply by 100 for the percent.

Answer: 43/5043 over 50, 0.860 point 8 6 and 86%

5. How far apart, and approximately equal

What you will be able to do

  • Find how far apart two numbers are, even when one is negative.
  • Decide which of several numbers is closest to a given number.
  • Read the symbols =, ≠ and ≈.
  • Round to one decimal place or the nearest whole number without rounding in steps, and carry when a 9 rounds up.

Distance between two numbers

How far apart two numbers are is the larger number minus the smaller one. A distance is never negative. When one number is negative, subtracting it adds its size: the distance from −3negative 3 to 4 is 4 − (−3) = 74 minus negative 3 equals 7. On a number line, that is 3 steps up to 0 and 4 more steps to 4.

Three symbols

=
"Equals." Exactly the same value, such as 0.5 = 1/20 point 5 equals 1 over 2.
≠
"Does not equal." Not exactly the same value, such as 0.49 ≠ 0.50 point 4 9 does not equal 0 point 5.
≈
"Approximately equal." Close, but not necessarily exact, such as 3.98 ≈ 43 point 9 8 is approximately equal to 4. A rounded value is approximately equal to the original.

Two numbers can be approximately equal and not equal at the same time. "Approximately" means close, not exactly the same.

Rounding, and three slips to watch for

Find the last digit you will keep. Look only at the one digit right after it. If that digit is 5 or more, add 1 to the kept digit. If it is 4 or less, leave the kept digit alone. Then drop the rest.

  • Subtracting in the wrong order. 4 minus 6 is negative 2, but the distance between 4 and 6 is 2.
  • Rounding in steps from the right. 2.4492 point 4 4 9 to one decimal place is 2.42 point 4. Rounding to 2.452 point 4 5 first and then to 2.52 point 5 is a slip.
  • A 9 that rounds up. When a 9 goes up by 1, it becomes 0 and adds 1 to the digit on its left. 6.956 point 9 5 to one decimal place is 7.07 point 0.

Worked example: Which is closer to 20?

Which is closer to 20: 18.618 point 6 or 21.221 point 2?

  1. 20 − 18.6 = 1.420 minus 18 point 6 equals 1 point 4Distance from 18.6 to 20: larger minus smaller.
  2. 21.2 − 20 = 1.221 point 2 minus 20 equals 1 point 2Distance from 20 to 21.2: larger minus smaller.
  3. 1.2 < 1.41 point 2 is less than 1 point 4The smaller distance wins.

Answer: 21.221 point 2 is closer to 20, even though it is above 20
