2. The F-Distribution and Requirements

These checks cover topics 1 and 2 on page 1 of the Section 9.4 notes.

Check 1 (The F-Distribution). Which description matches the properties of the F-distribution in topic 1?
Show answer

Right-skewed and not symmetric, its values cannot be negative, and its shape depends on the degrees of freedom of each sample.

3. Testing a Claim When Summary Statistics are Given

These checks cover topic 3 and the Example (When Summary Statistics are Given) on pages 1 to 4 of the notes, the video "Testing a Claim about Two Population Standard Deviations with Summary Stats" on page 4, and Classroom Example 9.4.1 on page 9.

Use this situation for Checks 4 and 5. A city compares wait times for two bus routes. A sample from Route 7 has a standard deviation of 4.64 point 6 minutes, and a sample from Route 12 has a standard deviation of 6.36 point 3 minutes. The claim is that the standard deviation of wait times for Route 7 is less than the standard deviation for Route 12.

Check 4 (Population 1). Following Step One in the notes, which route is population 1?
Show answer

Route 12, because it has the higher standard deviation.

Where this is: Step One and its notes in the Example (When Summary Statistics are Given) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Testing a Claim about Two Population Standard Deviations with Summary Stats" on page 4.

Check 5 (Alternative Hypothesis). With Route 12 as population 1 and Route 7 as population 2, which alternative hypothesis matches the claim that Route 7 has the lower standard deviation?
Show answer

H1: σ1 > σ2 (claim)H 1: sigma 1 is greater than sigma 2, claim

Where this is: Step One and its notes in the Example (When Summary Statistics are Given) on page 2 of the notes, and the video "Testing a Claim about Two Population Standard Deviations with Summary Stats" on page 4.

Check 6 (StatCrunch Conversion). In a different problem, the alternative hypothesis is H1: σ1 ≠ σ2H 1: sigma 1 is not equal to sigma 2. Using the conversion table on page 3, how is it written in StatCrunch format?
Show answer

H1: σ1/σ2 ≠ 1H 1: sigma 1 over sigma 2 is not equal to 1

Where this is: The Conversion Process for Null and Alternative Hypothesis table on page 3 of the notes.

Check 7 (Entering a Variance). In the StatCrunch Variance box for Route 12 (standard deviation 6.36 point 3 minutes), what should you type?
Show answer

6.3^26 point 3 caret 2, which is 6 point 3 squared

Where this is: The Big Reminders in the StatCrunch steps on page 4 of the notes, and the video "Testing a Claim about Two Population Standard Deviations with Summary Stats" on page 4.
